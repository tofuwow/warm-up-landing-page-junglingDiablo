About don't doubt!

The junglig diablo is a circus instrument made by two sticks, a rope that brings together the two sticks, and the clessidra.

One technique to use the diablo is by placing the clessidra in front of the right foot (if you are right-handed) and make it roll to the other side using the sticks (the rope must have some pressure because of gravity).

Once you arrive on the left foot, you must pick it up, here the magic happens, maintain the left hand straight and the right on motion, is like fishing!

Voilà the magic! The diablo moves!
